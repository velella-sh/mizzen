- **Dated data (design).** `docs/design/dated-data.md` fixes the date kinds (day, month, year), the as-of date and its order (`--as-of`, then `asOf`, then a collection's `referenceDate`), and what a feature reports when no date is set.
- **Partial dates.** `src/dates/parse.ts` reads `YYYY-MM-DD`, `YYYY-MM`, and `YYYY` with a precision, gives the earliest and latest day a partial date can mean, and rejects impossible dates such as `2026-02-30` with the position of the value, without `Date` strings or a time zone.
- **Formatting dates.** `src/dates/format.ts` writes a date in `en`, `fa`, or `ar` with month names, weekday names, and digits from built-in tables, in the Gregorian or the Jalali calendar (a fixed arithmetic conversion in `src/dates/calendars.ts`), and never uses `Intl`, so the output does not change with the Node or ICU version.
- **Documenting dated data.** `site/data/dates.md` covers the as-of date and its precedence, `dates.calendar`, date fields, `derived` counts and bands, the `:field[…]` formats, and the daily rebuild recipe; `test/data-dates-docs.test.ts` checks the schema keys and the navigation and guide links.
- **The as-of date.** `asOf: YYYY-MM-DD` in the site config and `--as-of <date|today>` on `build`, `check`, and `watch` set the day the build pretends it is; the flag wins. `--as-of today` reads the system date once at the CLI edge and prints it; nothing below the CLI reads a clock. A feature that needs the date and finds none reports `dates.no-as-of` at the place that needs it. `dates.calendar` (`gregorian` or `jalali`) picks the calendar dates are written in.
